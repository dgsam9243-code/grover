    Before I start listening

    • I use your microphone to notice sound and speech, and your camera (if it is on) to notice movement and people.
    • I read faces and body language to describe expressions (like smiling), not feelings. Faces are never stored or identified.
    • This is checked on this device. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded. The conversation summary is wiped when you close Grover.
    • Live captions use your browser's speech service, which may send audio to the browser's company to turn it into text.
    • Grover is a support tool. It is not a medical device and can make mistakes.

    Your brain is taking in a lot right now: sounds, lights and people. It has run out of space.

    You are not doing anything wrong. This place is just a lot for your body right now.

    Breathe in…

    1. Feel your feet on the floor.
    2. Name 5 things you can see.
    3. Notice 4 things you can feel, like your clothes or the chair.
    4. Listen for 3 sounds.
    5. Notice 2 things you can smell, and 1 you can taste.

    Go slowly. It's okay to stop after any step.

    1. Press your palms together hard. Count to 10, then let go.
    2. Squeeze your hands into fists, then open them slowly.
    3. Or push against a wall, or wrap your arms around yourself and squeeze.

    Do it 3 times. Pressure helps your body settle.

    You have full permission to leave. You can step outside, go somewhere quiet, or go home.

    You don't owe anyone eye contact or small talk right now.

    You could say “I need a quick break. I'll be right back.” “I'm going to head home now. Thanks for having me.”
