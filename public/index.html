    Before I start listening

    • I use your microphone to notice sound and speech, and your camera (if it is on) to notice movement and people.
    • I read faces and body language to describe expressions (like smiling), not feelings. Faces are never stored or identified.
    • This is checked on this device. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded.
    • Live captions use your browser's speech service, which may send audio to the browser's company to turn it into text.
    • Grover is a support tool. It is not a medical device and can make mistakes.
